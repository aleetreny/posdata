# Posdata

## Product facts from the brief

An original, richly informative, interactive atlas of career destinations after a PhD, for people from every discipline. Combine public data and prior research, explain real options, enable easy statistics, and make all results traceable. The user authorized a new GitHub repository and full public deployment, with a complete visual and functional review.

## Audience and central task

Doctoral researchers and graduates asking: where do people in my discipline work, what kinds of roles exist, and what evidence supports those paths? Start with exploration, then give the denominators and study limitations needed to interpret the answer.

## Implementation decisions

These are agent decisions within the brief, not preferences expressly selected by the user. Name: Posdata. Spanish interface with an English option. Static React/TypeScript app built with Vite and published on GitHub Pages. Versioned public data; no login, tracking, third-party analytics, or paid services. A link can retain a chosen view and filters. Export filtered rows. Sources remain individually attributed and their licenses are separate from the app license.

## Visual commitment

A scholarly career atlas: a terracotta masthead, warm paper working surface, dark brown typography and narrow sign-like headings. The signature is a continuous ledger from doctoral institution to employer, with expandable dated source evidence. Ochre is a locator, not decoration. The statistical context keeps its branching data diagram. Dense evidence uses rules, aligned columns and a continuous page rather than identical cards. Archivo Narrow for display, Source Sans 3 for reading. No photographic imagery is needed for this statistical tool.

## Editorial contract

Separate planned destinations at graduation, observed employment, and historical placement records. Never combine overlapping populations into a global person count. Employer labels do not prove a permanent contract. Missing values remain missing; the page names suppressions, age, geography and survey denominator. All broad disciplines can be explored; finer detail and countries vary by source. Aspirations to global completeness are not a verified world-ranking claim.

## Working surface

Trajectory explorer → evidence → statistics and career options → studies and methods. The default is doctoral origins in Europe with employment anywhere in the world, and worldwide origins remain directly available. Discipline and text search lead; origin and destination countries are optional independent filters. Mobile uses a persistent four-item bottom navigation and an accessible full-section dialog. Deep methods and datasets remain available without obstructing the main task.

## September 2026 scope revision

The user explicitly rejected the first edition's entry through four national collections and asked for extensive real extraction, prioritising doctorate holders trained in Europe and their employment worldwide, with US and other origins retained. They explicitly requested easier mobile navigation and a non-blue theme. The terracotta implementation is an agent choice within that authority. No new comp, aesthetic interview or exact shade approval is claimed.

The ORCID 2025 public archive is the primary individual source. Completed research doctorates and dated later job starts are required. Profiles are voluntary and incomplete; no population probabilities or current-job verification is claimed. ROR identifies organisation types, while discipline and role are transparent text rules. University placement lists remain separately searchable and are never added to ORCID person counts. A complete checksum-verified archive is a release requirement; provisional development extracts cannot be published.

The constrained mobile adaptation exposes the live filtered count and a direct “Ver trayectorias” link in the first viewport. The job-selection control and collapsible distributions follow the result heading, while the archive date, local-preview label (until extraction is complete) and stale-employment caution remain visible. This correction inherits the established ledger world; it is not a new concept selection. The initial code-led surface selection has no recorded concept-roll seed and is not represented as full compliance with that Impeccable process checkpoint.

The application code license does not override source rights.

| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ORCID Public Data File 2025 | [ORCID, official annual archive](https://doi.org/10.23640/07243.30375589), snapshot 2025-10-01, CC0 1.0. Extracted public professional education and employment fields only; no contact data. Do not confuse the bulk archive's licence with separate public API terms. |
| Research Organization Registry | [ROR v2.12, schema 2.1](https://zenodo.org/records/22099990), 2026-08-25, CC0 1.0. Organisation types and identifier/name matching enrich the ORCID records. Name-derived categories and identifier conflicts are documented separately. The reported job country is retained; test fixtures use records from this same CC0 edition. |
| Oxford Philosophy, Glasgow MCMP, Università Cattolica Science | Factual, attributed entries from public institutional placement lists. No open licence was established for the source pages; source prose and photographs are not republished. These lists retain their own rights and are not relabelled CC0. URLs, retrieval dates and source HTML hashes are in `university-careers.json`. |

Snapshots are dated 2026-09-19. Each downloaded source has a URL and SHA-256 in [the manifest](public/data/manifest.json). Downloaded background research is distinguished from integrated app data: TRaCE, Aarhus and UCSD appear in the research acquisition manifest but their complete documents are not served by the app.

Retrieval dates do not replace observation dates: the ORCID snapshot is October 2025, and institutional pages may include historical or future appointments. The separate [trajectory manifest](public/data/trajectories/manifest.json) records the full official archive checksum, extraction counts, classification rules and hashes of every compressed browse and detail shard. An incomplete local development extract cannot pass the release script. ORCID records, institutional entries and the Economics archive are not summed into one claimed unique-person total.

The directory includes resources whose microdata require an agreement or whose reports restrict commercial reuse. Directory inclusion is not a sublicense or an assertion that those resources were imported.
